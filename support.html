Stripey Support

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Stripey is a golf scoring app for playing games with your group — you set up a round, add your players, and everyone's scores stay in sync as you play.

Need help? Email mjbobak@sweetplumsstudio.com and we'll get back to you. You can also send feedback from inside the app: Me → Support.

Common questions

How do I sign in?

Stripey signs you in with Apple, Google, or an email address. Whichever you pick, you'll be asked once for a display name and an optional profile photo, and then you're in.

How do I start a round?

Tap to start a round from the Home tab, pick your course and the games you want to play, then add your players. Playing partners who don't have Stripey can be added as guests — you enter their name and score for them from your own phone, so the whole group doesn't need the app.

Everyone has the app. Do we each score separately?

You can. When players in a round are on Stripey, scores sync between your devices as they're entered, so anyone in the group can keep the card and everyone sees the same numbers.

What is Stripey Pro?

Stripey Pro is an optional subscription that unlocks the full set of game formats. Games that need it are marked with a lock. The current price and any free-trial offer are shown in the app before you're charged, and billing is handled by Apple through your Apple Account.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us. Open Me → Manage subscription in the app, or go to Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep Pro until the end of the period you've already paid for.

I already paid, but Pro isn't showing up.

Open the Stripey Pro screen and tap Restore purchases, and make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account you bought it with. If it still doesn't appear, email us and we'll sort it out.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I turn notifications on or off?

Notifications tell you when a round you're in starts or when scores change. Toggle them under Me → Notifications, or in your iPhone's Settings → Stripey.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Me and tap Delete account at the bottom of the screen. This permanently removes your profile and personal data. If you'd rather we did it for you, email the address above.

I found a bug, or something scored wrong.

Please tell us — golf games have a lot of local variations and edge cases. Send it through Me → Support in the app, or email us. If you can include the course, the game format, and roughly which hole it happened on, that helps a lot.
